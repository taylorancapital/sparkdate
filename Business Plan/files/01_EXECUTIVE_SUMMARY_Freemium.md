## THE SOLUTION

**Product:** IRL dating platform combining:
1. **Digital matching** (algorithmically matched attendees)
2. **Curated events** (60+ events/month by Year 1)
3. **Venue partnerships** (22-27 paid partner venues)

**Revenue Model (Freemium):**
- **User Tickets:** Gender-based pricing (women $0-22, men $25-35)
- **Premium Subscriptions:** $34.99/month ($50-70/month value)
- **Venue SaaS:** Free M1-6, then $299/month M7+ (80-85% adoption expected)

**Why This Works:**
- Venues get free customer acquisition platform
- See immediate ROI ($10K+/month per venue)
- By M7, switching cost is high; accept $299/month subscription
- SparkDate avoids operational complexity of F&B tracking
- Network effects lock in venue dependence

## WHY FREEMIUM VENUE MODEL

### vs. Charge Day 1 ($299/month from M1)
- ❌ Only 30-40% venue adoption (high friction)
- ❌ Weak network effects (few venues)
- ❌ $1,794-2,392/month by M12
- ❌ Slow growth trajectory

### vs. Revenue Share on F&B (Charge % of food)
- ❌ Operational complexity (F&B tracking, disputes)
- ❌ Venue incentive misalignment
- ❌ Difficult to scale to new cities
- ❌ Hard to predict revenue

### Freemium (Free M1-6, then $299 M7+)
- ✅ 80-85% venue adoption (proven ROI)
- ✅ Strong network effects (22+ venues, 60+ events/month)
- ✅ $7,176/month venue revenue by M12 (scales to $70K+/month Year 2)
- ✅ Simple, predictable, scalable
