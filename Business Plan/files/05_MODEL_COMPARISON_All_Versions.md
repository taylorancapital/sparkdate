## PART 2: OPERATIONAL COMPLEXITY ANALYSIS

### 2.1 F&B Revenue Tracking (v2 vs v3)

#### v2 Model: 5% of Verified F&B

**Required Operations:**
1. **App Check-in System:** Attendees check in at event (QR code)
2. **F&B Spend Verification:** Venues track spending for each attendee (via check-in ID)
3. **Revenue Reconciliation:** Venues report F&B per check-in; SparkDate calculates 5% owed
4. **Payment Processing:** Monthly invoicing and payment collection from venues
5. **Dispute Resolution:** Venues question "why only $1,200 revenue when 40 people attended?"

**Complexity Factors:**
- Venues must report accurate F&B spend per attendee (incentive to underreport)
- App must track check-in with payment system (integration complexity)
- Monthly reconciliation required (accounting overhead)
- Potential disputes over F&B numbers (relationship strain)

**Hidden Costs:**
- $40-50K annual technology to build/maintain check-in + F&B tracking
- 0.5 FTE annual finance/ops to reconcile
- Potential revenue loss from disputes/underreporting
- Venue relationship strain (they feel monitored)

**Success Rate:** 
- ~70% of venues accurately report (others underreport)
- Expected F&B revenue = 70% × estimated = 70% × $107K = $75K actual (vs. $107K estimated)

---

#### v3 Model: $299/Month Subscription

**Required Operations:**
1. **Contract Signature:** Venue agrees to $299/month
2. **Payment Processing:** Monthly subscription billing (automatic)
3. **Reporting:** Provide analytics dashboard (attendees, F&B estimate, ROI)
4. **No Verification Needed:** SparkDate doesn't care about actual F&B spend

**Simplicity Factors:**
- Venues self-report F&B for their own benefit (transparent reporting)
- Billing is automated (Stripe subscription)
- No app integration with payment system needed
- No monthly reconciliation
- No disputes over numbers

**Hidden Costs:**
- Minimal technology required
- Finance overhead: Near zero (automated billing)
- Relationship is clean (partnership, not revenue sharing)

**Success Rate:**
- 85%+ of venues stay (high ROI is obvious; $299 is cheap)
- Zero disputes (SaaS model is industry standard)
- Expected venue revenue = 85% × (18-24 venues × $299 × 6 months) = $29.5K-$35K actual

**Translation:** v3 generates $652K cash in Year 1; v2 generates $381K cash (because of F&B operating costs). v3 is $271K more profitable despite lower revenue.
