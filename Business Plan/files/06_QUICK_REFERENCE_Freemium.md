## THE PITCH (60 SECONDS)

**Problem:** 78% of dating app users experience burnout from swipe-based platforms. Gen Z & Millennials want real-world connection, not endless messaging.

**Solution:** SparkDate combines algorithmic matching with curated IRL events. Users attend 1-2 events per month; venues get paid customer acquisition.

**Business Model:**
- **Users pay:** $25-35 per event (gender-based), $35/month premium subscription
- **Venues pay:** FREE (M1-6), then $299/month (M7+) once addicted to SparkDate traffic

**Traction:** 50K+ users, 24 paid venue partners by Month 12

**Financials:** $1.2M Year 1 revenue, $652K EBITDA (55% margin), breakeven Month 6

**Ask:** $100-130K seed for 12 months to profitability

## THE FREEMIUM VENUE MODEL

### M1-6: Free Platform (Prove ROI)

**What Venues Get:**
- Event listing on SparkDate platform
- Attendee curation (verified, screened users)
- Marketing (Instagram, email, word-of-mouth)
- Trained event host
- Check-in system + analytics

**Venue Profit:**
- 40 attendees × $35 F&B spend = $1,400 revenue
- Room cost discount = $150
- Net profit per event = **$1,250**
- 8 events/month = **$10,000/month profit**

**Venue Mindset:** "SparkDate brings me $10K/month. This is amazing."

---

### M7+: Paid Subscriptions ($299/month)

**The Conversation:**
"You're making $10K/month from SparkDate. We're now charging $299/month. That's 3% of your profit."

**Expected Result:**
- 82% conversion (ROI is too obvious to pass up)
- 18% churn (easy to replace with new venues)
- By M12: 24 paid venues × $299 = $7,176/month revenue

**Year 2 Venue Revenue:** $70K-90K/month (240%+ growth)
