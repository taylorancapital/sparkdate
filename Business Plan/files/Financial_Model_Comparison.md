## WHY THE IMPROVEMENTS?

### 1. Premium Subscription Revenue Surge (+104%)
**Original model:** 10% conversion, neutral on gender
**Revised model:** 8% female (high), 5% male (low)

Female users subscribe more because:
- Lower CAC → higher lifetime value (ROI on subscription makes sense)
- Better retention (45-50% vs. 25-30% default)
- Higher perceived value (early event access, inner circle)
- Feel safer/more invested in platform

**Result:** 3,450 subscribers (vs. 1,500) by M12 = $120.7K/month (vs. $52.5K)

---

### 2. Lower CAC (-21%)
**Original model:** Assumed $13 blended; only 60% from communities
**Revised model:** $10.20 blended; 70% from communities

Reasons:
- Female referrals cheaper (women recruit women)
- Influencer channels more efficient for female acquisition
- Community partnerships work better with gender-based discount
- Less reliance on expensive paid ads

**Result:** $2.80 lower per customer = $156K annual savings across 56K users

---

### 3. Better Retention & Repeat Attendance
**Original model:** 40-50% repeat attendance assumed
**Revised model:** 50%+ repeat attendance achieved

Why:
- 50%+ female = safer events = women stay longer
- Male attendees enjoy better gender ratio = come back
- Network effects kick in earlier = community forms
- Premium features (early access, inner circle) drive engagement

**Result:** Higher ARPU ($57 vs. $50), higher subscription conversion

---

### 4. F&B Upside (Small but Meaningful)
**Original model:** 10% of F&B; estimated ~60-70% participation
**Revised model:** 5% of F&B; but 85%+ check-in rate guaranteed

Net impact:
- Lower per-person revenue BUT higher participation
- More importantly: Engagement signal (check-in = commitment)
- Venue data more accurate = better partnerships
- F&B credit incentivizes staying longer (more drink orders)

**Result:** $35K net F&B revenue (vs. $96K gross in original, after 10% cut)
