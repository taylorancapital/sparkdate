# Founding Member Recruitment Kit — SparkDate Philadelphia

**Goal:** Recruit 40-50 hand-picked founding members for first 4-6 events
**Why this matters:** These are not just users. They're the social proof, the gender ratio anchors, and the word-of-mouth engine for everything that follows.

---

## TARGETING PRINCIPLES

### Lead with women.
Industry standard is 65-75% male. Our model breaks if we don't reverse this. Every recruitment decision should be filtered through: *"Does this bring high-quality women into the founding cohort?"*

### Founding members are NOT customers.
They are co-conspirators. We give them:
- Free first 2 events
- "Founding Member" badge in app
- Direct line to founder (you)
- Voice in event format/venue selection
- First access to every new event

### Quality > Quantity.
Better to launch with 35 right people than 60 random sign-ups.

## QUALIFICATION FILTERS (Before Approving Founding Members)

Approve if:
- ✓ Real, complete profile
- ✓ Lives in Philly metro
- ✓ Age 22-38
- ✓ "Why I'm applying" answer is thoughtful, not generic
- ✓ Public LinkedIn or Instagram (verifies real human)

Decline (politely; "we'll be in touch as we open more spots") if:
- ✗ Generic application ("sounds cool")
- ✗ Missing info or fake-seeming
- ✗ Outside age range
- ✗ Far from city core
- ✗ Already has 5+ from same demographic in cohort (balance gating)

## RED FLAGS DURING RECRUITMENT — STOP EVERYTHING IF:

- 🚩 More than 60% of applicants are men → pause men, push female community partnerships hard
- 🚩 Applicants are clustered in one age (e.g., all 22-25) → diversify outreach channels
- 🚩 Founding members aren't showing up at events → screen harder, require small refundable deposit
- 🚩 Community organizers aren't responding → your pitch is wrong; rewrite
