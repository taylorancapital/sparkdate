- **Focus:** Community + Creator partnerships (primary), minimal paid ads
- **Targets:** 5-7 communities, 15-20 micro-influencers

- **Add:** 5 corporate partnership pilots
- **Users:** 15,000 registered, 5,000 MAU

- **Goal:** Achieve breakeven/modest profitability, LTV:CAC >4:1
