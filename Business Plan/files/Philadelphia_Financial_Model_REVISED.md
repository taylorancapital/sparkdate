### 1.2 F&B Economics Optimization
### (Increased from 10% to 5% per check-in, but with higher conversion)

#### The F&B Model Enhancement

**Old Model (Original Financial Model):**
- 10% of F&B sales (all attendees)
- Avg F&B spend: $25-36/person
- Revenue share: ~$3-3.60 per attendee
- Conversion: Relied on voluntary F&B tracking

**New Model (Optimized with Check-In Requirement):**
- 5% of F&B sales per check-in (lower %, but guaranteed participation)
- Avg F&B spend: $30-40/person (higher due to validation)
- Revenue share: $1.50-2.00 per attendee (but lower %)
- Conversion: 85-90% of attendees check in (vs. 60-70% assumed before)

**Why This Actually Improves Economics:**
1. **Higher F&B Spend:** Check-in = validated attendee → more drinks/food ordered
2. **Higher Participation Rate:** Check-in requirement captures everyone, not just some
3. **Better Venue Data:** Venues track F&B spend by SparkDate event (clean accounting)
4. **Incentive Alignment:** SparkDate benefits from attendees staying longer + spending more

---

#### Check-In Mechanics & F&B Incentive

**App-Based Check-In System:**
- Upon arrival at event, attendees scan QR code or tap "Check In" in app
- Check-in triggers: "You earned $2 credit toward drinks/food tonight"
- Attendee scans QR code when ordering (validates F&B spend)
- SparkDate tracks: Time in, drinks/food ordered, time out, total spend
- Venue integration: POS system links to SparkDate (real-time F&B tracking)

**Financial Incentive Structure:**
- SparkDate gives $2 F&B credit per check-in
- Cost to SparkDate: $2
- Attendee spends $30-40 on F&B
- SparkDate captures 5% of $30-40 = $1.50-2.00
- Net: -$0.50 to break-even per attendee on F&B credit
- But: Attendee stays longer, spends more, higher engagement → better venue relationship

**Venue Benefit:**
- SparkDate attendees spend $30-40/person (vs. $20-25 typical bar traffic)
- Venue benefits from higher F&B spend
- SparkDate handles customer acquisition + event curation (venue just provides space)
- Venue happy to reduce room rental discount in exchange for better attendance quality
