# SPARKDATE PHILADELPHIA: FINANCIAL MODEL (REVISED)
## 1.2K Users by M12 with 3-Tier Subscription Pricing

**Tier Structure:**
- **Free Tier:** $0/month (1 event/month) — Auto-upgrades to $9.99 after 30 days
- **Mid Tier:** $19.99/month (2-3 events/month)
- **Premium Tier:** $39.99/month (unlimited events)

**Conservative Conversion (M12):**
- 70% free tier (840 users)
- 20% mid tier (240 users)

## KEY ASSUMPTIONS

**User Acquisition:**
- M1-3: Organic + founding members (word-of-mouth)
- M4-6: Community partnerships + influencers
- M7-12: Viral + referral loops
- Target: 2K users by M12 (conservative)

**Event Metrics:**
- M1: 4 events/month × 30 attendees = 120 tickets
- M12: 80 events/month × 40 attendees = 3,200 tickets
- Average ticket price: $30 (gender-based pricing: women $20, men $40)

**Subscription Conversion:**
- Free → Mid (after 30 days): 30% convert
- Free → Premium (after 60 days): 10% convert
- Total paid users: 30% of base by M12

**Operating Costs:**
- Payroll: $40K/month (8 FTE: founder, 2 ops, 2 hosts, 1 dev, 1 marketing, 1 admin)
- Events: $8K/month (venue costs, supplies, host bonuses)
- Tech: $1.5K/month (Firebase, Stripe, hosting, tools)
- Marketing: $2K/month (ads, partnerships, content)
- G&A: $2K/month (insurance, legal, misc)
- Total: ~$53.5K/month by M12

## SENSITIVITY ANALYSIS

**If CAC is 2x higher ($20 vs. $10.20):**
- Year 1 EBITDA: $377,920 (-$120K)
- Still breakeven by Month 6-7
- Still fundable

**If conversion to paid is 20% (vs. 30%):**
- Subscription revenue drops 33%
- Year 1 EBITDA: $471,920 (-$26K)
- Breakeven shifts to Month 6-7
- Event tickets compensate

**If attend rate is 35% (vs. 40%):**
- Event ticket revenue -12.5%
- Year 1 EBITDA: $442,420 (-$55.5K)
- Breakeven shifts to Month 6-7
- Still viable

**Key insight:** Event tickets are the lever. Subscription is nice-to-have upside.
