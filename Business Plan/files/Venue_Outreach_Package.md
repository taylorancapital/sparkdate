## F&B ESTIMATE — read before quoting any number

**There is no measured F&B figure.** Attendees open separate tabs, so the spend
lands in the venue's POS and never in ours; we see ticket revenue only. No venue
has ever reported a total back. Every figure below is a **projection**, and the
only grounded input is Tellus at **$18-22 a head** (about three drinks, plus tip
— and tip is staff money, not the owner's F&B line).

Per-head spend is a property of **the venue**, not of SparkDate. A dive bar and
Parc cannot produce the same number, which is why one fixed figure must never be
quoted to every venue.

**Range: $500-$2,000. Typical mid room at 30 guests: ~$1,050.**

In outbound copy, hand the multiplier to the operator — they know their own check
average, and at a $50 room it lands them above anything we would have asserted.

- Pure nightclubs (too loud for conversation)
- Sports bars (wrong vibe)
- Fast-casual / counter-service (no dwell time)

## OBJECTION HANDLING — THE TOP 7

### "We don't host third-party events."
*"This isn't a third-party event in the traditional sense — there's no rental fee, no production costs to you, and no exclusive buyout. We promote your space on a slow night, drive 25-40 paying guests, and they buy from your normal menu at normal prices. You keep 100% of food and beverage revenue. Worst case, you have a quiet Tuesday made busier."*

### "What's the catch? Why is this free?"
*"We make money from the consumers — they pay for tickets and a small subscription. Our economics work because we deliver high-quality groups, not because we charge venues. Charging venues is what kills similar models. We're betting that if we make this great for you, you'll keep the slot, recommend us to other venues, and eventually upgrade to a paid tier when we add value beyond foot traffic."*

### "What if your people are weird / underdressed / loud?"
*"Every attendee is identity-verified and pre-screened. We host on-site for the first 30 minutes. Your bartenders and managers retain full discretion to refuse service to anyone who isn't a fit — voucher or no voucher. Our brand depends on your brand. We can't afford to send the wrong crowd."*

### "What kind of crowd?"
*"Philadelphia young professionals, ages 25-34, household incomes $60K-$100K+, college-educated, gender balanced. Think: Hinge users who got tired of Hinge. We curate aggressively — most events skew 50%+ female, which is unusual and means more balanced spending."*

### "Who handles the alcohol liability?"
*"You do — same as any normal night. We don't sell or distribute alcohol. We don't issue drink vouchers in Pennsylvania (PA prohibits free drink promos at licensed venues). Attendees buy directly from your bar at your normal prices. We provide host insurance naming you as additional insured. You retain all your normal protections."*

### "How often?"
*"We start with one night per month. If both sides are happy after two events, we move to weekly or bi-weekly on a recurring slot. We never want to be on your busy nights — only the ones where we're additive."*

### "What do we have to do?"
*"Almost nothing. Confirm a date and time. Brief your team that we're coming (we send a one-pager). Set aside a corner or back room for our intro circle if possible. That's it. We handle promotion, RSVPs, check-in, host facilitation. You pour drinks and serve food."*
