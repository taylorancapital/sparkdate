FAQ

Quick answers.

How is this different from Tinder Events?

Tinder Events is bolted onto a swipe app. We start with the event. Matching is the means to a great evening, not the end product.

What does it cost?

Signing up is free. Events range from $25-150 depending on venue and tier. Premium subscription ($34.99/mo) is optional.

Where in Philly?

Launching in Rittenhouse first, expanding to Old City, University City, and Fishtown over Months 4-6.

What about safety?

Identity verification required. Trained hosts on-site. Clear code of conduct. Venues retain full discretion. Safety is non-negotiable.

Is it gender-balanced?

We aggressively target 50/50. Most dating apps are 65-75% male. We curate every event to be balanced or female-majority.

What ages?

Primary focus is 25-34, with secondary cohort 22-28. We balance demographics within each event.
