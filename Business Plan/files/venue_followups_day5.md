## Tips when sending:

1. **Wait until day 5-7** after the initial email. Earlier feels pushy.
2. **Send between Tue-Thu, 10am-2pm.** Highest open rates for restaurant managers.
3. **Personalize the [Name]** field. If you don't know the name, "Hi team" beats "Hi there."
4. **Limit to 1 follow-up.** If they don't respond to follow-up #1, move on. Try them again in 90 days with a fresh angle.
5. **Reply to your own original email** (don't start a new thread). It bumps the conversation and gives them context.
