Questions?

What's SparkDate? +
SparkDate is an experience-first dating platform. Instead of infinite swiping, we host curated events in real Philly venues where you meet compatible people. No algorithm tricks. No pen pals. Just real conversations that lead somewhere.
When's the first event? +
We're launching in Q3 2026 (early July). Founding members get first access—we'll email you the exact date and venue in June. You'll know before anyone else.
Why should I sign up now? +
Founding members get free entry to the first two events (valued at $60), lifetime founding member status, and direct access to the founder. You're not just a user—you're helping build the product. That's special.
What if I hate the first event? +
No hard feelings. We're still figuring out what works. Your honest feedback helps us improve. You get one more free event to try again, and we listen to what didn't work.
How many founding members are there? +
We're capping the founding cohort at 50 people. It's exclusive because we're building this together—smaller group, more real feedback, more weight to your voice.
