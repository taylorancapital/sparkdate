What's SparkDate? +
SparkDate is an experience-first dating platform. Instead of infinite swiping, we host curated events at real Philly venues — where the guest list is algorithmically matched by intent, vibe, and interest before you walk in, and the morning after, we exchange contact info with anyone you both clicked with. No pen pals. No missed signals.
