What a singles getaway with SparkDate looks like

We host singles mixers in Philadelphia and Lancaster — small, curated rooms where a host handles the introductions and roughly 70% of attendees leave having exchanged contact info with someone. Getaways take that exact formula and stretch it across a weekend: a group small enough that you meet everyone, one hosted dinner or activity to break the ice on night one, and shared spaces — a firepit, a big kitchen table, a pool deck — that keep the group naturally together.

That structure is the difference between a singles trip and a solo trip that happens to have other people on it. Scenery doesn't introduce people; hosts do. If you've ever come home from a group tour having spoken to almost no one, you already know why this matters.

From Poconos cabins to a singles cruise

The shortlist ranges from close-to-home weekends — a cabin retreat in the mountains, a spa resort weekend — to bigger swings like a singles cruise, Palm Springs, and Taos. If you're weighing a nearby escape first, our guide to weekend getaways for singles near Philadelphia covers what's within two hours of the city and how to make it social.

Getaway questions, answered

What is a SparkDate Getaway?

A multi-day trip for a small, curated group of singles — the same philosophy as our mixers (real people, introductions handled by a host), stretched across a weekend or longer. Think shared cabin retreats, spa weekends, or a singles cruise, with a group small enough that you actually meet everyone.

When do they launch?

They're in the planning stage now. We build the packages in order of demand — the trips with the most votes on this page get organized first. Leave your email on a package you vote for and you'll be the first to hear dates and pricing.

How does voting work?

Click "I'm Interested" on any package — no signup required. Each vote counts toward that package's total, and the totals decide what we build first. After voting you can optionally leave your email to get first dibs when that trip opens.

Is this only for people near Philadelphia and Lancaster?

Our mixers are Philadelphia and Lancaster based, and the first getaways will be organized from that community — but the destinations range from the Poconos to the Pacific, and singles from anywhere are welcome to vote and join the list.

Do I need to be a SparkDate member?

No. Like our mixers, getaways will be open to any single who books a spot. Attending a mixer first is a great way to meet the community the trips are built around, but it won't be required.
